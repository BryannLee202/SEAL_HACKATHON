# Cot: PHUONG_THUC  DUONG_DAN  BTC  GK  MENTOR  TS  KHACH
#   BTC   = coordinator@demo.local   (Ban to chuc)
#   GK    = judge1@demo.local        (Giam khao)
#   MENTOR= mentor1@demo.local
#   TS    = leader@demo.local        (Thi sinh / doi truong)
#   KHACH = khong dang nhap
#
# Gia tri:
#   CHO = duoc vao   -> ma tra ve KHONG duoc la 401/403
#   CAM = bi chan    -> ma tra ve PHAI la 401 hoac 403
#   Luu y ve CAM: Spring MVC giai va kiem tham so cua phuong thuc TRUOC khi
#   chay chot @PreAuthorize. Nen goi bang than rong {} hay thieu tham so bat
#   buoc thi nguoi khong du quyen nhan 400 chu khong phai 403 — chot quyen
#   chua kip chay. Phep chan van con nguyen (gui dung dinh dang thi dung
#   403), va khach chua dang nhap van bi chan 401 ngay o tang filter. Vi vay
#   bo kiem coi 400/415 la KHONG KET LUAN DUOC va dem rieng, chu khong bao
#   loi; con 2xx hoac 404 thi bao loi that, vi ca hai deu co nghia than
#   phuong thuc DA chay, tuc chot quyen khong he ton tai.
#
#   SAU = chan o tang service theo tai nguyen cu the (vi du: chi doi truong
#         cua DOI DO, chi giam khao duoc phan cong VONG DO). Phep quet goi
#         bang UUID khong ton tai nen luon cham 404 truoc khi toi phep kiem
#         — khong ket luan duoc, bo qua o day va da co unit test rieng.
#         Rieng cot KHACH van la CAM that su: Spring Security chan truoc.
#
